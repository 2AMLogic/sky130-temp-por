## Injection (instrument, disclosed departure from the uninstrumented assembly)
- **Status**: INSTRUMENT, not part of the design. The two lines are present in every class-A deck (the no-injection control keeps them with gen = 0, so the control has the same topology and an idle, zero-current element) and absent from every class-B deck, whose DUT netlist is byte-identical to the committed export.
- **Elements**: `BINJ INJD VSS V=v(POR_RAW)*(1-v(ginj))`; `RINJ INJD POR_RAW <rinj>`
- **Mechanism**: A Thevenin source behind a finite resistor RINJ into the por_comparator output node POR_RAW (the node the deglitch dwell filters). The source voltage tracks v(POR_RAW)*(1-g) where g(t) in [0,1] is the global drive GINJ (testbench BGINJ, a trapezoid: rise te, flat width tw, fall te, starting at t0). With g = 0 the source equals the node voltage, so the element draws exactly zero current at baseline (no idle loading); with g = 1 POR_RAW is pulled toward VSS through RINJ and the comparator output stage (XMI2P/XMI2N) keeps driving the node -- the injected level is therefore a divider of RINJ against the comparator pull-up, not an imposed voltage.
- **Loading**: RINJ (amplitude axis), the target level is VSS (0 V), no capacitance is added. The comparator, output chain, bias and temp_core stay connected and unmodified; the netlist diff against the committed DUT is exactly the two lines above plus a .save of the RINJ current (checked by selftest).
- **Limit rule**: if injection prevents a physically meaningful operating point (nonphysical node, supply current reversal, injection never takes POR_RAW below V_MID, or POR_RAW pinned below V_IL) the point is reported NONPHYSICAL/UNRESOLVED with the injection fields recorded; as-drawn immunity is not claimed from it.
- **Measured per point**: current through RINJ (i(@r.xdut.rinj[i])), POR_RAW minimum, POR_RAW dwell below V_MID are recorded per point; the sampled GINJ drive is checked against the declared trapezoid (tolerance 0.02) or the point is UNRESOLVED.

## Measurement definitions (exploratory; none is a ratified bound)
- **levels**: V_IL = 0.2 vf, V_IH = 0.8 vf, V_MID = 0.5 vf, V_MUST = 0.5 vf (vf = the point's starting supply)
- **assertion**: RESETn falls through V_IL starting from HIGH at t0 (Schmitt).  assertion_rail_limited is recorded when VDD itself was <= V_IL at that instant (the output could not stay high regardless of the comparator).  Release = RESETn rises through V_IH.
- **detectability**: a dip is 'required-to-assert' when VDD spends >= 50 us below V_MUST (piecewise-linear dwell from the full-resolution VDD).  Dips that are not required may assert or not -- both are recorded, neither fails -- but an assertion must still recover.
- **expectation**: Per stimulus, stated in every point: class A from the full-resolution POR_RAW dwell below V_MID -- < 50 us (D_REJ) calls for REJECTION (RESETn must stay above V_IL), >= 1 ms (D_REQ) calls for ASSERTION (must assert, then release), in between assertion is optional but must release; no-injection control calls for no assertion. Class B from the VDD dwell below V_MUST -- >= 50 us (T_REQ) calls for assertion, otherwise optional; rail_collapse (VDD <= V_IL) and dwell_rejection_visible (POR_RAW fell, RESETn did not assert) are recorded. D_REJ/D_REQ are exploratory definitions, not claims about the design's dwell.
- **baseline**: viable when, before t0: exactly one RESETn release and no re-assert, RESETn >= V_IH and POR_RAW >= V_MID over the 2 ms before t0, delivered bias branch current > 0 with peak-to-peak <= 5 % of its mean. Otherwise UNRESOLVED (detected codes are kept in 'detected_codes_ungraded').
- **recovery**: VDD recovered when >= 0.98 vf after the recovery edge starts; an assertion beginning later is reassert_after_recovery; two or more assertions are reassert_chatter; RESETn must be >= V_IH over the last 1 ms.
- **delivered bias**: explicit measurement: drain current [id] of bias_core XMPIB (PMOS whose drain IS the IBIAS node), saved by the testbench as .save @m.xdut.xbias.xmpib.msky130_fd_pr__pfet_g5v0d10v5[id] and appearing in the klt waveform as i(@m.xdut.xbias.xmpib.msky130_fd_pr__pfet_g5v0d10v5[id]). Sign: ngspice BSIM4 reports id POSITIVE for normal conduction in both polarities (established by the brownout-top local probe: XMPIB id ~0.78 uA equals the sum of the consumer diode devices' ids), so the delivered branch current is +id and must be positive at baseline (else NONPHYSICAL). The consumer-side diode devices (comparator, output chain, temp_core XMBD [id]) are saved; their sum over the delivered current at the end is recorded. The IBIAS NODE voltage is recorded separately and never used as a current. End/baseline ratio band [0.8, 1.25] else FAIL bias_not_recovered.
- **resolution**: max sample gap from t0-10 us to glitch end+100 us must be <= min(20 us, width/5, edge/8) for class A (0.1 % float tolerance) and the brown-out rule min(20 us, hold/5, edge/10) for class B AND the sampled drive (VDD for B, GINJ and VDD for A) must match the declared stimulus (VDD within 20 mV, GINJ within 0.02); class A additionally needs the injection to take POR_RAW below V_MID (else the dwell was not exercised -> UNRESOLVED). Otherwise a point with no detected failure is UNRESOLVED, not PASS.
- **physicality**: every saved node within [-0.3 V, vf+0.5 V] for t>0, settled supply current -i(BVDD) > 0, delivered bias positive at baseline. Violation -> NONPHYSICAL (cannot PASS; behavioural verdict not graded).
- **verdicts**: ERROR (missing waveform / channel / non-finite data / convergence, model or backend error) > NONPHYSICAL > UNRESOLVED (unviable baseline, truncated, sparse, stimulus mismatch) > FAIL (resolved physical failure, with trace evidence) > PASS.  NOT_COVERED marks points whose request was never submitted, was refused by the fleet, or has no report; they are counted as uncovered, never as PASS.
- **deck attribution**: each point's retrieved input deck must carry this experiment's banner and request tag, the committed XMN1 L=20 W=0.42, no diagnostic marker, the class's injection element exactly when the class is por_raw_inject, and the declared .param line; otherwise ERROR deck_attribution (a point can never be credited to the wrong DUT/class).
- **stimulus**: Common: power-up ramp min(vf, rup*t) from a physical 0 V, then glitch at t0. Class vdd_level: V(VDD) = min(vf, rup*t) - ex*(clip01((t-t0)*s/ex) - clip01((t-t0-ex/s-th)*s/ex)), ex = max(vf - vlow, 0), symmetric slews sf = sr = s, hold th at the floor (the brown-out BVDD source with sf = sr; SPICE twin of brownout_checker.vdd_expected). Class por_raw_inject: V(VDD) = min(vf, rup*t) only; GINJ g(t) = gen*(clip01((t-t0)/te) - clip01((t-t0-te-tw)/te)). Width tw is the FLAT-TOP width; the full pulse is tw + 2te at the base and tw + te at half amplitude. Control: gen = 0 (class A) / vlow = 99 (class B).
- **observation**: 8 ms after the glitch ends (class A: end of the GINJ fall edge; class B: end of the recovery edge, at the largest starting supply): 1.9x the slowest observed release delay (4.3 ms). A shorter record is truncated -> UNRESOLVED unless a failure is already detected.
- **grid**: RINJ is the amplitude axis: 1 kohm against the comparator's W=2 L=0.5 pull-up is a deep pull-down, 30 kohm a shallow one (levels factor 30 apart; the achieved POR_RAW minimum is MEASURED per point, not assumed). Flat widths 20 us - 2 ms (two decades) straddle the checker's D_REJ = 50 us and D_REQ = 1 ms expectation thresholds on the measured POR_RAW dwell. Edge time is varied one factor at a time around the center point (10 us, 200 us around the 20 us default) -- a declared one-factor-at-a-time treatment, not a full cube. Faster edges (< 10 us) need tmax < 1.25 us and a per-request cost estimate first.
- **expansion policy**: No envelope is claimed. Expansion is a NEW record, never an edit: (a) where a PASS<->FAIL (or reject<->assert) boundary appears between adjacent levels on any axis, add the midpoint level on that axis at the affected corners only; (b) edges faster than 10 us (class A) / slews above 200 kV/s (class B) need a cost estimate first; (c) widths outside 20 us - 2 ms, RINJ between/outside {1k, 30k}, floors between the three levels only with a stated reason; (d) repeated glitches, glitches before the baseline is released, ll/hh passive skew and mismatch Monte Carlo are not in this increment. Any post-hoc addition is labelled as such in its record.
- **trace storage**: committed traces are change-triggered (dV >= 2 mV on VDD/RESETn/POR_RAW/IBIAS node, delivered bias or injection current moved >= 2 % of its maximum, GINJ moved >= 0.01, or a 1 ms anchor); RESETn assertion/release counts are re-derived from the stored trace and compared with the full-resolution result per point.
