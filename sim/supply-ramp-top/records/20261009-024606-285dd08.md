## Measurement definitions (exploratory; none is a ratified bound)
- **levels**: V_IL=0.2*vf, V_IH=0.8*vf, V_RUNT=0.1*vf, V_MID=0.5*vf
- **release/re-assert/runt**: rising V_IH crossing of RESETn (Schmitt, starts LOW); re-assert = falling V_IL crossing while HIGH; runt = excursion above V_RUNT that returns without reaching V_IH; independent V_MID up/down crossing counters
- **premature**: first release precedes POR_RAW's first V_MID rise (or POR_RAW never rises)
- **resolution**: max sample gap <= 20 us (= minimum detectable glitch width) and sampled VDD within 20 mV of min(vf, rate*t); otherwise a point with no detected failure is UNRESOLVED, not PASS
- **physicality**: every saved node within [-0.3 V, vf+0.5 V] for t>0 and settled supply current -i(BVDD) > 0; the t=0 sample (degenerate all-zero-supply solve) is reported separately
- **quasi-static**: Half-rate paired segment. 'consistent' when the VDD at which POR_RAW rises agrees within the tolerance (threshold-style measurement); 'censored' when POR_RAW rises after the ramp has ended (VDD already at its final value, so no VDD threshold is measured); 'rate-dependent' otherwise. The POR_RAW->RESETn release DELAY is a time-domain quantity (one-shot), so it is graded on the full/half ratio, not on VDD. Tolerances: VDD@POR_RAW within 0.05 V; delay ratio in [0.8, 1.25].
- **rate/window**: V(VDD) = min(final_supply, rate*t) from a physical 0 V start; ramp duration = final_supply/rate, so each rate is independent of the swept final supply (no fixed-duration confound, gf180 DR-021). tstop = 3.63 V / rate + 15 ms hold after the longest ramp of the request. Pilot runs (tt/27C/3.3V and ss/ff/fs/sf at -40 and 125 C, 2.97 V) released 1.7-3.0 ms after POR_RAW rose, so 15 ms of hold is ~5x the slowest pilot release. A point that does not release within the window is reported as 'no_release within window', not as a proven non-release.
- **trace storage**: committed traces are change-triggered (dV>=2 mV on any of VDD/RESETn/POR_RAW/IBIAS, or 1 ms anchor); the transition counts are re-derived from the stored trace and compared with the full-resolution result per point (`stored_trace.agrees_with_full_resolution`).

## Measured follow-on recommendation
Measured follow-on recommendation (non-binding; no numeric bound is ratified by this record). (1) The as-drawn circuit cannot be simulated with the pinned PDK because por_output_chain.XMN1 (L=25) has no valid model bin; all 360 as-drawn points are reported as ERROR. A design issue should decide the XMN1 geometry (the L=20 diagnostic here is not a design change). (2) On the diagnostic variant, the transition checker found one release and no re-assert at 358 of 360 points; the two transition FAILs are at 500 kV/s and are listed in the points table (one no_release within window, one re-assert after release); they should be re-run at finer time resolution before being attributed to the circuit. (3) A large share of points are FAIL on physicality only, because the temp_core self-biased nodes (xtemp.nb, ptat/ctat) go far below -0.3 V at the t=0 solve and for some period after; this is the #86 startup artifact and must be resolved (or its model-level cause identified) before any threshold from this campaign is trusted. (4) Release delay after POR_RAW is rate-independent within the half-rate guard for all paired points with a release (full/half ratio in band), so the ramp-rate dependence is in where POR_RAW rises, not in the one-shot delay. (5) Next increments: the ll/hh passive-skew cross product, rates below 500 V/s and above 1 MV/s with a dedicated time-step strategy, and brown-out, as separate scopes.
