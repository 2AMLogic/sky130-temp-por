## Method

Reproduce with `python3 -I sim/temp-core-accuracy-mc/hand_calc_20261017.py` (standard library plus one
single-corner local `ngspice` `.op` per point, three points total; the Monte Carlo is not re-run).
It reads gm/Id of the mirror and amplifier devices and the PTAT loop voltages from the `.op` on the committed
snapshot `netlist-snapshots/20261011-165907-69d13ac/nominal.spice`, evaluates the closed-form sigmas
below, and prints them beside the sigma of the same quantity over the record's per-sample CSVs
(`corners/20261011-165907-69d13ac/samples-<term>.csv`, valid samples only).

**Error definition checked** (README, record "Error definition"): `err(T) = (PTAT_sample(T) - PTAT_nominal(T)) / S`,
`S` = same-corner nominal full-range slope = 4.710 mV/degC (tt/3.30 V) and 4.707 mV/degC (sf/2.97 V). The
script divides by the same `S`, so hand and MC are normalised identically. The record's normalisation is the
plain one for a degC-referred voltage error; nothing odd found.

### Loop algebra (temp_core)

`R1` carries `x = I2 R1 = Vt ln(8 I1/I2)`; `PTAT = I3 (R2 + R2TRIM)`, so the nominal gain
`PTAT/x = (R2+R2TRIM)/R1 = 25.97` (the MC amp samples regress dPTAT on Vos at 25.9 V/V: the same).

- **Amplifier**: input-referred `sigma(Vos)^2 = 2 s_MI^2 + 2 (gmL/gmI)^2 s_ML^2`, `s_MI = 12 mV/sqrt(4 x 32)` = 1.06 mV
  (MI1/MI2, pfet L4 W32), `s_ML = 8.2 mV/sqrt(8 x 8)` = 1.03 mV (ML1/ML2, nfet L8 W8). `dPTAT = 25.97 Vos`.
  1 mV of Vos is 5.5 degC, which is why the amplifier is a problem at any realistic area.
- **Mirror**: legs MP1/MP2/MP3 (pfet L4 W8, I = 0.443 uA at tt/27 C) with `delta_k = (gm/Id) dVth_k`
  (14.35 /V, so 3.04 % 1-sigma per leg). With the loop closing `x` on `I2`:
  `dI3/I3 = d3 - d2 + c (d1 - d2)`, `c = Vt/x = 0.475`, giving `sigma = 1.84 sigma_delta` and
  `dPTAT = PTAT dI3/I3`. Cascode mismatch (MPC1..3) is neglected (second-order through Vds).
- **PNP**: `sigma(dVBE) = Vt sqrt(1 + 1/8) 2.16e-3` (Q1 vs the 8-parallel Q8 array; IS only, bf neglected), x gain.
- **Resistor**: `PTAT ~ (R2+R2TRIM)/R1`, relative sigma `sqrt(r1^2 + (w2 r2)^2 + (w2t r2t)^2)` with
  the 4.64e-2/sqrt(W L) values and length-share weights.

Device sizes and operating points are from `design/netlist/temp_core.spice` and the `.op` below.

## Verdict, per term

- **Mirror and amplifier (the two dominant terms): confirmed.** Hand is 0.77 to 1.03 of the MC at all three
  points, inside the issue's "small factor" and the curator's <= ~2x. The amplifier hand figure is within 20 %
  at tt and 3 % at the worst corner; the mirror is a consistent 20 to 23 % low. A per-corner sigma from
  n = 28 to 30 samples carries about 13 % 1-sigma sampling error (record "Statistical resolution"), and the
  hand calculation neglects the cascode Vds modulation and the exponential nonlinearity of `delta`, which
  may account for part of the residual (not separated here).
- **PNP and resistor (one-line checks): consistent.** Resistor 0.85 to 1.10; PNP 0.49 to 0.87. The PNP hand
  figure is IS-only (the model's `bf` mismatch also acts) and falls short of the MC at 125 C and at the
  worst corner, but the term is about 0.1 % of the total variance (0.9 degC against 25.8 degC at the worst corner), so the shortfall cannot change the verdict.
- **Total**: hand RSS 19.9 degC vs MC 24.4 to 25.8 degC (0.77 to 0.81).

The 3-sigma headline is therefore a real consequence of the circuit's device sizes and of the PDK's
mismatch constants, not an artefact of the campaign: a PTAT-type sensor with 54 to 72 mV of delta-VBE converts
1 mV of amplifier offset into 5.5 degC, and the mirror legs run at gm/Id = 11 to 14 /V (moderate inversion
at 0.44 to 0.59 uA), so 2.1 mV of per-leg Vth mismatch becomes a 2.3 to 3 % current error that passes straight
into PTAT (1 % of PTAT = 3.0 degC).

## Limits

- Hand calculation at three (corner, T) points, not all 15 corners; each is an independent single-corner
  `.op`, not a Monte Carlo.
- The amplifier is treated as `Vos` at the input referred through gm ratio only (second-stage and cascode
  contributions neglected); the same applies to the mirror (cascode neglected). The `other` term (0.26 degC
  worst corner in the record) is not hand-checked; it is two orders of magnitude below the dominant terms.
- Global process Monte Carlo, layout/gradient mismatch, dynamic behaviour: as in the record's "Not measured".
- `MC_PR_SWITCH` is 0 in the campaign, so no absolute resistor/PNP spread is in either side of the comparison.
